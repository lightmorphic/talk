Speak anywhere.
Your voice never leaves your machine.

Talkin is dictation for the Linux desktop. Hold a key, speak, let go — your words appear wherever your cursor is. Recognition runs entirely on your own computer.

Install Talkin View on GitHub

Free & open source · MIT · No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry

One key. Three moments.

  1. 1

    Hold

    Hold Right Ctrl (or any key you choose) in any app — email, editor, browser, terminal.

  2. 2

    Speak

    A small circle shows a live waveform while Talkin hears you, and a revolving spinner while it thinks.

  3. 3

    Let go

    Your words are typed where your cursor is, cleaned up and correctly spelt — in about a third of a second.

Built for people who think faster than they type

Private by design

Speech recognition runs on your CPU with NVIDIA's open Parakeet model. After install, the app is pinned hard-offline — unplug the internet and Talkin won't notice.

Teach it your words

Highlight any word it mistyped, press the correction key, type the right spelling once. Never see the mistake again. Export your dictionary and take it anywhere.

Cleans up after you

Ums, uhs and false starts are removed before the text lands. What you meant, not every noise you made.

Works everywhere you type

It types like a keyboard, so every app is covered. Push-to-talk or toggle, your choice of key, any microphone.

Speaks your language

Every word of the interface lives in one spreadsheet-friendly file. Six languages included; add your own with one column.

Yours, permanently

MIT licensed. History, settings and dictionary are plain local files with one-click export. Updates come straight from GitHub — when you ask, never behind your back.

The whole privacy policy, in full

Talkin sends nothing, to no one, ever. There is nothing else to disclose.

No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no cookies on this site. The only network access the app ever makes: downloading the speech model when you install, and checking GitHub for updates when you open Settings.

Install

git clone https://github.com/lightmorphic/talkin
cd talkin
python3 -m venv --system-site-packages .venv
.venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
./scripts/talkin.sh

Linux with X11 · Python 3.11+ · GTK 3 · First run downloads the speech model (~600 MB), then you're offline for good. Full details in the README.
