Cookies

Last updated 6 September 2026.

We do not use cookies

This website sets no cookies of any kind: no essential cookies, no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies. That is why you have not been shown a cookie banner. There is nothing to consent to and nothing to opt out of.

Nor other storage

The site does not use local storage, session storage or any similar means of keeping information in your browser. It does not fingerprint your device.

What the site does load

  • The pages, stylesheet and font, served from this domain.
  • The Lightmorphic apps launcher, a script from apps.lightmorphic.com, which we operate. It draws the grid of dots in the top bar and lists our other applications. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and sends nothing about you.

Nothing else is fetched from anywhere else. There are no third-party embeds, no advertising and no analytics.

The application

Lightmorphic Talk is a desktop application, not a website, and uses no cookies. What it stores on your own computer, and nothing leaves it, is described on the privacy page.
